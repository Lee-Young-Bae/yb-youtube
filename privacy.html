Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

What this app is

YB Youtube is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app uses these Google permissions:

  • Upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (youtube.upload)
  • Read the owner's channel and video information (youtube.readonly)
  • Read the owner's YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly)

The app cannot delete or edit videos, read or post comments, or change channel settings.

How data is stored and used

Sign-in tokens and channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are used only to upload the owner's videos and to review the owner's channel performance.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access stops the app immediately.

Contact

Questions can be filed as an issue at github.com/Lee-Young-Bae/yb-youtube/issues.
